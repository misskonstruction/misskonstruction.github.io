Hope Sneaks In Quietly

July 18, 2026

If you’ve been following along lately, you know this blog has been a little heavy.

Life has been… a lot.

A missing cat. An aging dog whose future weighs on my heart every single day. Health struggles. Moving. Family stress. The constant feeling that I was one more inconvenience away from completely unraveling.

Those feelings were real. They still are.

But this morning?

This morning felt different.

Not dramatically different. There wasn’t some magical sunrise or life-changing phone call. Hope didn’t come crashing through the front door waving pom-poms.

It just… quietly showed up with a cup of coffee.

I did something I rarely let myself do. I made my coffee, climbed right back into bed, put my Pink Bose Petals (earbuds to normal people) in, and finished watching Exit 8. Based on the wildly popular Japanese video game, the film follows a man trapped in an endless subway passage where the only way out is to notice tiny abnormalities around him. Miss even one, and you’re sent right back to the beginning. It’s psychological, unsettling, confusing, and honestly one of the most fascinating movies I’ve watched in a long time. If mind-bending psychological thrillers are your thing, I highly recommend watching the trailer first.

🎬 Trailer: https://www.imdb.com/title/tt35222590/?ref_=ext_shr_lnk

The funny thing is… the movie accidentally fit my life a little too well.

Sometimes it feels like I’ve been walking the same hallway over and over, convinced I’m making progress only to find myself back where I started.

But unlike the movie, life does eventually hand you an Exit 8.

You just have to keep walking.

And today, I didn’t feel guilty for taking that slow start. I needed it.

Once I got up, I opened the household chore chart my lovely AI assistant, Roland, helped me create. (Seriously… if you have ADHD or your brain occasionally resembles a browser with 74 tabs open, having someone break overwhelming tasks into manageable pieces is life-changing.)

Instead of staring at my beautiful new home wondering where to even begin, I finally had a plan.

One room.

One task.

One little victory at a time.

Then came another unexpected gift.

My son’s car isn’t nearly as damaged as we feared from the way he described it, and my partner believes he can fix it.

Huge relief.

Even better… I apologized to my son.

Not one of those half-hearted “I’m sorry you were upset” apologies.

A real one.

He accepted it with so much grace and love that I honestly think we’re closer today than we’ve been in a long time.

That’s worth more than any repaired car.

And now, sitting here looking around this house, I’m smiling because another thought keeps sneaking into my head.

Yes… this is my beautiful house.

(If you caught that Talking Heads reference, congratulations. We can be friends.)

I’m still ridiculously excited to start my next oil painting today. That blank canvas has been calling my name for days.

But somehow, I think it’ll feel even better after I’ve shown this home a little love first.

There’s something satisfying about earning your creative time.

Taking care of the place that’s taking care of you.

Building a home before creating art inside it.

So if you’ve found this blog because you’ve been reading through my darker days, I want you to know this too:

Hope isn’t always loud.

Sometimes it arrives wearing pajamas, carrying coffee, watching weird Japanese psychological thrillers, apologizing when it’s needed, cleaning a kitchen one counter at a time, and realizing that life might actually be turning a corner.

I’m still me.

I’ll still have bad days.

I’ll still write honestly about them.

But today…

Today feels good.

And I refuse to forget to write about those days too.
