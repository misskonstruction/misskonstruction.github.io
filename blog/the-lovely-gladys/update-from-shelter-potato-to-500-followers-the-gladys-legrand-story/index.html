Update: From Shelter Potato to 500 Followers — The Gladys LeGrand Story 🐾💗

November 8, 2026

Hello, everyone!

My name is Gladys LeGrand, and apparently I have a blog now.

Mother says I was supposed to have written something here two weeks ago, but I’ve been very busy being adorable, supervising household operations, stealing expensive pillows, and developing my social media empire.

You know. Important shit.

Anyway, since some of you don’t use Instagram, I thought I’d introduce myself properly and explain how a formerly homeless bulldog became a professional bed thief with 500+ followers, a growing fan club, and an actual career in the works.

It’s been a pretty wild couple of months.

Once Upon a Time, I Was a Shelter Girl

Before I became Gladys LeGrand, I had a different name, a very different life, and absolutely no idea what was waiting for me.

I’d been abandoned, had already experienced motherhood, and was pregnant again when the nice people at Partners for Pets in Marianna, Florida, took me in.

Things weren’t exactly going my way.

But the people at the shelter cared about me, and somewhere along the way, I got to work with an inmate trainer who taught me some very useful manners and commands.

I was a good student.

Mostly.

Then something wonderful happened.

A lady found me through an app called TREAT, where people play games to help shelter dogs. She kept playing for me, sending treats, medication and food packages to me via the game and eventually decided she couldn’t live without me. Less than a month later, the adoption was approved!

On October 4, 2026, I officially became somebody’s dog.

Not just somebody’s dog, either.

I became Gladys LeGrand.

And my new Mother had absolutely no idea what she’d gotten herself into.

Welcome to the Circus

My new house came fully equipped with a large, comfortable bed, a backyard, a personal chef, a brother named Kylo, and an assortment of cats with questionable attitudes.

Especially Jasper.

Jasper was not impressed with my arrival. Apparently, Grampa Jasper is seldom impressed or amused by much of anything.

I tried to explain that I was a sweet, gentle girl who simply wanted to be everybody’s friend. Jasper apparently needed time to ponder. During this time he accidentally misplaced one of his claws. In my face.

Eventually though, he came around. We even cuddled, which Mother photographed because she seems to think everything I do is breaking news.

Kylo is my dog brother. He’s a little weird, but I love him. Mother says the best part of this home is that everyone is weird in their own unique way.

There are other cats, too. Lots of them. I haven’t figured out why we need so many, but Mother seems committed to the collection.

And then there’s the bed.

Omg, the bed.

It’s enormous, adjustable, and has wonderful pillows.

I also discovered that I can fit underneath the bed, although getting back out occasionally requires assistance.

Mother calls me a baked potato.

I have decided this is a term of endearment.

My First Adventures as a LeGrand

Once I settled in, Mother started taking me places.

We went riding in her Toyota Tacoma, which she calls Mina. At first, getting into the truck was a whole production involving ramps and assistance.

Then I figured out I could jump in myself.

Problem solved.

We went shopping at the pet store, where I discovered that humans have entire buildings dedicated to things dogs might enjoy.

What a magnificent civilization.

I got treats, toys, and a whole lot of attention.

We also went exploring outside, including a nature preserve where Mother helped rescue a turtle.

I supervised.

Very important job, supervising.

Back home, I discovered that Mother makes our dog food herself. Chicken, rice, vegetables, all kinds of delicious things.

Apparently she also makes fish sometimes, but certain recipes cause unfortunate atmospheric disturbances.

I will not be taking questions about the farting.

Then Mother Gave Me an Instagram Account

On October 16, Mother decided the world needed to see more of me.

Naturally.

She created an Instagram account called Gladys Unsupervised, which is a little misleading because I’m almost never unsupervised.

In fact, I follow Mother everywhere.

The bathroom? Yes.

The office? Absolutely.

The kitchen? Obviously.

If she gets up, I get up. If she sits down, I supervise. If she lies down, I become a weighted blanket.

Personal space is a human invention I do not recognize.

My account started with just a handful of followers and a couple of posts.

Mother shared pictures and videos of me doing what I do best: being myself.

No fancy staging. No pretending I’m something I’m not. Just a real rescue dog living her very real, occasionally chaotic life.

And apparently people liked that.

My Musical Career, Sort Of

There’s something else you should know about me.

I am a Haute & Freddy girl.

Mother plays their music all the time, and now it’s the soundtrack to my Instagram adventures.

Every video. Every Reel. Haute & Freddy.

I even got my very own shirt!

The lovely people behind the music noticed me, and Mother was practically bouncing off the walls.

Personally, I was just excited about having another thing that smelled like Mother.

But I understand this was a big deal.

I also starred in a rather scandalous couch video set to their song Scantily Clad.

Mother says I’m not allowed to have an OnlyFans account. I guess that world is missing out on this fabulous bod.

Friends, Fans, and Famous Farts

The followers started arriving.

First a few. Then a few more.

We celebrated 50. Then 100. Then 200.

I made friends with dogs all over the place, including pit bulls in pajamas, tiny fancy dogs, and even cats who apparently enjoy watching a bulldog make questionable decisions.

One day, Partners for Pets started sharing my adventures with their own followers.

The same people who had helped me find my forever home were now helping the world get to know me.

Before we knew it, I had 379 followers.

Mother kept staring at her phone.

I kept asking for bacon.

We all have priorities.

Meanwhile, companies started noticing me, too.

One sent me a very fancy, super-quiet nail grinder after Mother talked about how my nails had been cut too short before I came home.

The grinder has lights and different attachments, and Mother says we’re having a spa day.

I sniffed it, snorted, and returned to my snack.

I believe this demonstrates excellent product-testing skills.

Nurse Gladys Reporting for Duty

One of my favorite humans is my brother Seth.

He got sick with a nasty cold, so naturally I took it upon myself to provide medical assistance.

My treatment plan involved cuddling him, lying on him, and generally making sure he couldn’t go anywhere.

Mother took pictures.

Of course she did.

Apparently my bedside manner is exceptional.

And that brings me to something really important.

I’m Going to Service Dog School!

Mother has some serious health challenges, and we’ve decided that I’m going to learn how to help her.

I’m officially training to become a medical alert service dog.

My job will involve learning to recognize signs of medical distress, including changes in her heart rate and breathing.

I already pay close attention to Mother. Now, with professional training, I’ll learn how to turn that attention into useful skills.

My human has a coach helping us, and my formal lessons began yesterday.

Mother says I’m highly trainable.

She’s right.

I already learned lots of commands from my inmate trainer, and Mother has been teaching me new ones.

For example, when she pretends to shoot me with her fingers, I dramatically collapse onto my back with my legs in the air and my tongue hanging out.

It’s called playing dead.

I call it theater.

I’m hoping my coach appreciates the performing arts.

The important thing is that Mother and I are learning together, and I’m preparing for a job that means an awful lot to both of us.

She gave me a home. Now I’m learning how to help take care of her.

I think that’s a pretty good arrangement.

There Have Been Some Minor Incidents

I should probably mention that not every adventure has gone exactly according to plan.

For instance, Mother recently sat outside making strange animal noises at me.

I was very interested.

So interested, in fact, that I decided to jump into her lap.

Unfortunately, Mother was sitting in a lawn chair.

And apparently lawn chairs have weight limits.

We went backward.

Both of us.

The video ended rather abruptly.

Mother used several words that I am not permitted to repeat on Instagram.

I maintain that I was simply demonstrating affection.

No further questions.

And Then There Were 500! 🎉

On November 7, 2026, something amazing happened.

I reached 500 Instagram followers!

FIVE HUNDRED!

Mother got a notification with a little trophy and everything.

She made me a beautiful celebration video with hearts, sparkles, and my picture in our garden at night. Here is the still version. You’ll have to check the reel out on Instagram. Mother said something about coding this site all herself and hosting it on gitHub and something else called “bandwidth” she’s already over-used. Okay…by all means make this page load more efficiently by sacrificing my sparkles.

I looked very distinguished though, right?

Like a dog who pays taxes.

Which I absolutely do not.

In just a few weeks, my little account had grown from a handful of people into a community of 500 friends, with 34 posts and around 3,800 views over the previous 30 days.

And the nicest part?

People seem to love me exactly as I am.

Not because I’m perfect.

Not because I know fancy tricks.

Not because I’m becoming a service dog.

They love watching me snore, cuddle cats, ride in Mina, make Mother laugh, and occasionally cause minor structural incidents involving lawn furniture.

I’m just Gladys.

A once-abandoned shelter dog who found a family, a very comfortable bed, and a whole lot of people who think I’m worth watching.

This Is Only the Beginning

Mother says this little corner of her website is where we’re going to collect my adventures for people who aren’t on Instagram.

There will be stories about my family, my training, my ridiculous daily activities, and whatever else happens when a bulldog takes over a household.

Mother also has her photography here on MissKonstruction, so you can look around and see what else she creates when she’s not busy documenting my magnificence.

She’s pretty talented.

I say that because she’s my Mother, but also because I have seen the pictures.

For now, I just want to say thank you.

Thank you to Partners for Pets for giving me another chance.

Thank you to the people who have followed my adventures, shared my pictures, and cheered me on.

And thank you to Mother for choosing me.

I think we both needed each other.

Now, if you’ll excuse me, I have a very important appointment with a pillow.

Love,

Gladys LeGrand 🐾💗

Former shelter girl. Professional bed thief. Aspiring medical alert dog. Social media potato.
